Support

Stuck, found a bug, or want your data gone? Write to qurangen.us@gmail.com — every mail is read by the developer.

Download the games

Inkblade on Google Play · Pixel Space Dodge on Google Play

The game won't install / update

Install only from Google Play. If an update stalls, clear the Play Store cache, check free storage (100 MB+), and retry. Never sideload an update signed by anyone else — Play binds each app to its original signing key.

I lost my progress

Both games save locally on your device. Progress survives updates but not uninstalls or "clear data". There are no accounts, so nothing can be restored from a server — keep device backups on.

Delete my data

There is nothing to request: we hold no accounts and no servers. Uninstall the app (or clear its data) and every trace — saves, settings, purchase flags — is gone from your device. Ad data is held by Google; opt out via Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Ads / purchases (Pixel Space Dodge)

Rewarded ads are always optional. A one-time Remove Ads purchase disables interstitials forever; rewarded placements stay available. Restore purchases from Settings → Restore Purchases on a new device with the same Play account.

Report a bug

Include: game name, app version (shown on the menu screen), device model, Android version, and what happened step by step. Screenshots help.
